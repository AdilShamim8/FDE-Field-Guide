# Interviews

How FDE hiring works and how to prepare for every round. The loop is unusual: it tests
production engineering and customer instinct at the same time, and most candidates fail on
the second half.

- [The interview process](01-interview-process.md) - loop structure and stage-by-stage patterns
- [Coding and technical rounds](02-coding-and-technical.md) - what the coding round actually tests
- [System design rounds](03-system-design.md) - customer-flavored system design
- [Customer scenario rounds](04-customer-scenarios.md) - role-plays and discovery exercises, with rubrics
- [Behavioral rounds](05-behavioral.md) - the stories you need ready
- [Take-home assignments](06-take-homes.md) - formats and evaluation rubrics
- [Question bank](07-question-bank.md) - question patterns by round type

Interviewers are evaluating engineering judgment, customer empathy, communication, and
ownership - the same qualities as the job itself. Prepare by doing the job's shape of work,
not by memorizing answers.
