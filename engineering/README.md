# Engineering

The build-side depth an FDE needs: shipping code into environments you did not set up,
against systems you do not own, under constraints you did not choose.

- [Prototyping and PoCs](01-prototyping-and-pocs.md) - proofs of concept that produce decisions
- [APIs and integrations](02-apis-and-integrations.md) - the craft of gluing your system to theirs
- [Data pipelines](03-data-pipelines.md) - the unglamorous 80% of most engagements
- [Cloud and infrastructure](04-cloud-and-infrastructure.md) - deploying into existing AWS, Azure, and GCP estates
- [Security and compliance](05-security-and-compliance.md) - passing the reviews that gate every production deployment

These documents assume you can already build software. The FDE-specific difficulty is not
the building - it is the building inside someone else's constraints. Pair this section with
[working in customer environments](../customer/03-working-in-customer-environments.md).
