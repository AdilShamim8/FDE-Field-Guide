# Deployment

Getting from a working prototype to a system a customer's business depends on. This is the
gap the FDE role exists to close - and where most AI pilots die.

- [Prototype to production](01-prototype-to-production.md) - why pilots stall and how to plan the crossing
- [Deployment patterns](02-deployment-patterns.md) - VPC-embedded, SaaS-adjacent, and hybrid patterns with trade-offs
- [Production readiness checklist](03-production-readiness-checklist.md) - the go/no-go list before any launch

The MIT NANDA research found roughly 95% of enterprise GenAI pilots deliver no measurable
P&L impact. The difference between the 5% and the 95% is rarely model quality. It is
everything in this section.
