# Troubleshooting

Debugging as an FDE is its own discipline: production systems you did not write, in
environments where you cannot see everything, while a customer watches.

- [Debugging methodology](01-debugging-methodology.md) - a structured approach under time pressure
- [Debugging customer systems](02-debugging-customer-systems.md) - working through logs, tickets, and other people's process
- [Common failure modes](03-common-failure-modes.md) - the recurring failures and their fixes

The fastest trust builder in an FDE engagement is calm, structured debugging when something
breaks in production. The fastest trust destroyer is flailing. These documents exist to
prevent the flailing.
