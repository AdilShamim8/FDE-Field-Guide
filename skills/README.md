# Skills

The skill set that separates working FDEs from engineers who are good at one half of the
job. Every document here pairs the skill with the failure modes you get when you skip it.

- [Core technical skills](01-core-technical-skills.md) - the technical baseline, weighted by employer demand evidence
- [Discovery and requirements](02-discovery-and-requirements.md) - turning conversations into requirements without inventing any
- [Communication and storytelling](03-communication-and-storytelling.md) - making technical work legible to the people paying for it
- [Stakeholder management](04-stakeholder-management.md) - aligning people who have different definitions of success
- [Ambiguity and prioritization](05-ambiguity-and-prioritization.md) - deciding and cutting scope when nothing is fully specified

Technical depth gets you into the room. The other four skills are why customers ask you
back. Treat them as engineering skills, not soft skills.
