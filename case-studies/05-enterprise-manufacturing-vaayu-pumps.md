# Manufacturing field-service evidence review

This file reviews the evidence required for the previously presented Vaayu Pumps manufacturing case. Reviewed on 2026-10-09. No cited source artifact in this checkout authenticates the claimed deployment, private operating volumes, architecture implementation, or commercial outcomes; this is not a verified production field study.

## Claims withdrawn from observed evidence

The earlier figures for installed pumps, customer sites, technicians, depots, weekly complaints, triage reduction, first-time fix rate, and liquidated damages have no reproducible measurements here. Neither a reference to an unavailable document pack nor a video title establishes those figures. They must not be copied into market research or a portfolio as achieved outcomes.

The described four-agent system, SAP middleware, supervisor memory, and confidence threshold are an unverified design outline, not capabilities implemented by ETISE. ETISE contains no SAP connector, agent orchestration, persistent learned memory, or manufacturing dataset.

## What a real case record needs

- A public primary deployment report or permission to publish the customer's account, with date and exact source location.
- A definition and observation window for each business metric, denominator, baseline, and exclusion.
- Evidence of actual asset and work-order integration, including authorization, retries, idempotency, and failure recovery.
- A privacy and rights review for operational records, with retained source IDs and approved transformations.
- Independently labeled evaluation and operator acceptance, with failures and uncertainty visible.
- A handover owner and tested operating procedures tied to the delivered system.

Do not substitute invented site exports or asserted supervisor interviews for missing evidence. Until these inputs exist, treat the architecture as a proposal and keep the claimed numerical outcomes unavailable.

## Engineering questions to retain

We recommend examining asset identity consistency, parts availability freshness, authorization for work-order changes, offline technician workflows, duplicate dispatch, and escalation ownership. These are design-review questions, not claims about the named business.

Before adopting multi-agent orchestration, compare it with a deterministic workflow on authorized real records. A fixed confidence score is not a calibrated safety threshold, and recording supervisor edits does not automatically establish a learning system.
